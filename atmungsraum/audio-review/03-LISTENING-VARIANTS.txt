ALTERNATIVE LISTENING VERSIONS: WHAT WOULD SOUND DIFFERENT
Snapshot: 2026-10-27. These are not the current browser sequence.

CURRENT SELECTED-VOICE AUDITION (documented package absent in this checkout)

The newer ElevenLabs package uses the same saved British Breathing Instructor
profile as runtime, with this 16-second cycle:

00:00-00:04  "Einatmen … zwei … drei … vier."
00:04-00:08  "Halten!" [once, followed by quiet]
00:08-00:12  "Ausatmen … zwei … drei … vier."
00:12-00:16  "Halten!" [once, followed by quiet]

Each inhale/exhale phrase comes from one connected synthesis take. The script
preserves native word speed and fits the phrase by changing gaps. Counts target
one-second beats but can be slightly delayed by a longer preceding word.
Prior project verification records inhale word starts at 0/1.056/2/3 seconds
and exhale starts at 0/1.164/2/3 seconds. These are historical observations,
not remeasured here. Adjacent JSONs would provide the exact authored frames.
Prior notes also record the single Halten! take as about 0.753 seconds of
speech followed by about 3.247 seconds of silence in each hold slot.

Within inhale/exhale, the voice/counts descend in level with targets
0/-4/-8/-12 dB. The breathing recording retains its own dynamics at a constant
gain, with 10 ms fades at its boundaries. Voice and breath have equal average
power over the four-second phase; their perceived loudness requires listening.

Three comparisons would be available in audio-assets/ready-to-listen/elevenlabs/:
  00-cycle-16s-voice.wav  Spoken cycle only, quiet after each Halten!.
  00-cycle-16s-breath.wav Recorded inhale, silent hold, exhale, silent hold.
  00-cycle-16s-mix.wav   Speech plus breath on both inhale and exhale.
There are also separate phase files. In a mixed 16-second audition, both moving
phases have breath. The runtime's every-third-cycle rule does not describe this
single-cycle audition file. The listening helpers do not create a full session
with welcome, long rests and ending, or select a policy for future deployment.

EARLIER LOCAL AUDITIONS (historical, not selected)

Original counted voice: Einatmen / Und halten / Ausatmen / Und halten.
Only inhale/exhale count two/three/four; holds have quiet tails.

Voice-to-breath crossfade: counted inhale and exhale voice descend while breath
rises through the phase; both holds say Halten once and continue quietly.
This rising-breath envelope differs from the newer constant-gain breath.

Counted hold variants:
00:00  "Einatmen … zwei … drei … vier."
00:04  "Halten … zwei … drei … vier."
00:08  "Ausatmen … zwei … drei … vier."
00:12  "Pause … zwei … drei … vier."
They have voice-only and mixed versions and count during both holds.
The user rejected OmniVoice versions; they must not be confused with the
selected ElevenLabs voice or silently promoted into the browser.
