All times count from the session's audio-clock start, after loading and the
default 250 ms scheduling lead. Deliberate pauses extend real elapsed time.
Quoted German is the saved speech script. Bracketed descriptions are review
notes and are NOT spoken. Ranges describe complete sample slots, including
their quiet tails, rather than the duration of continuous speech.

These files describe the checked-in sequencer and PCM assets. They are not a
recording, a new listening assessment, or verification of physical sound output.
The individual timelines are checked against the runtime JSON files under
audio/scenarios/ and the shared library manifest, with source hashes recorded
in their headers. JSON now owns playback timing/order; matching CSV timing
sheets accompany it. These text scores are a fixed snapshot: refresh them if
scenario JSON, the engine, selection mapping or sample set changes.
No new speech was synthesized and no existing audio was replaced for this review.

The newer local ElevenLabs listening package is documented but absent from
this checkout. Its description is based on its source scripts and folder guide;
the package itself was not inspected or auditioned in this pass.
