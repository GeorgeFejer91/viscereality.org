IF THE PARTICIPANT PAUSES OR LEAVES

Pause stops the current audio and freezes the countdown. Fortsetzen continues
at that same position; it adds no new welcome or spoken restart cue. Real
elapsed time becomes longer than the selected active duration. Beenden/Escape,
navigation away, a hidden page or an unexpected audio-clock interruption stops
the attempt without playing a special spoken farewell. A new Starten begins
a new sequence. On-screen loading/error/completion messages are not voice audio.

POINTS FOR LISTENING REVIEW

The welcome promises counting in every phase, but the holds are uncounted.
Is that clear enough to someone listening with eyes closed?
Does the silent main-round opening make sense without looking at the screen?
Do the repeated identical cues, intermittent breath overlays and rest return
phrases feel natural at their authored boundaries?
Compare the current "Und halten" with the separate "Halten!" audition before
deciding whether to replace the runtime samples.
